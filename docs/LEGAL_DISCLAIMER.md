## 2. Ethiopian Statutory Tax & Regulatory Framework

Selet incorporates default tax and regulatory parameters reflecting current Ethiopian proclamations and directives. Users are advised of the following specific limitations:

### 2.1 Value Added Tax (VAT 15%)
- **Statutory Authority:** Value Added Tax Proclamation No. 1341/2016 (repealing and replacing Proclamation No. 285/2002), as administered by the Ethiopian Ministry of Revenue (MoR).
- **Default Rate:** Standard rate of **15%** on taxable supplies of services and digital deliverables.
- **Limitations & Exceptions:**
  - Mandatory registration applies only to businesses meeting or exceeding statutory annual turnover thresholds (currently 2,000,000 ETB, subject to Ministry directives).
  - Certain export software services supplied to foreign clients outside Ethiopia may qualify for zero-rating (0%) under specific Ministry of Revenue regulations, documentation rules, and foreign currency remittance proofs.
  - Selet provides a standard default 15% rate calculation; the application of exemptions or zero-rating must be confirmed by a licensed tax professional.

### 2.2 Domestic and Non-Resident Withholding Tax
- **Statutory Authority:** Federal Income Tax Proclamation No. 979/2016.
- **Domestic Services Withholding:** Standard **3%** withholding tax applies to payments made by withholding agents (entities designated by the Ministry of Revenue, including commercial banks, state-owned enterprises, and large corporate entities) for supply of services exceeding statutory transaction thresholds.
- **Cross-Border / Non-Resident Withholding:** Payments to foreign software vendors, cloud hosting providers, or overseas contractors may be subject to a **10%** non-resident withholding tax under Article 51 of Proclamation 979/2016, unless modified by bilateral Double Taxation Avoidance Agreements (DTAAs).
- **Cash Flow vs. Revenue:** Withholding tax is an advance tax deduction and cash-flow reduction withheld by the client at the point of disbursement. It is not an agency expense; it represents a credit against final annual corporate income tax liability.

### 2.3 Private Organization Employees' Pension (POESSA 11%)
- **Statutory Authority:** Private Organization Employees' Pension Scheme Proclamation No. 1268/2022 (repealing Proclamation No. 715/2011).
- **Employer Contribution:** Mandatory **11%** employer contribution calculated on the employee's basic salary.
- **Employee Contribution:** Mandatory **7%** deduction from the employee's basic salary.
- **Remittance Period:** Remittance must be executed monthly to the Private Organizations Employees Social Security Administration (POESSA) within thirty (30) days following the payroll month.
- **Scope:** In Selet, the employer pension (11%) is factored into the team member fully burdened direct labor cost. Users must ensure that statutory pension ceilings, probationary period rules, and expatriate exemption certificates are independently validated.

### 2.4 Foreign Exchange Rates & Currency Controls
- **Statutory Authority:** National Bank of Ethiopia (NBE) Directives, including Foreign Exchange Directive No. FXD/01/2024.
- **Rate Source:** Currency conversions derive from published National Bank of Ethiopia (NBE) benchmarks and commercial bank weighted averages.
- **Volatility & Currency Risk:** Foreign exchange rates in Ethiopia are subject to market conditions and regulatory circulars. Selet calculations assume spot exchange rates and do not hedge against forex fluctuations. Agencies quoting in foreign currencies (USD, EUR, GBP) or incurring cloud infrastructure costs in foreign currency are strongly advised to include appropriate risk contingency buffers.
